Tabler Icons
https://github.com/tabler/tabler-icons

Six outline glyphs vendored as inline <symbol> markup in
docs/design/web-ui-mockup.html: refresh, copy, external-link (used as
"open"), check (used as "confirm"), search, plus (used as "add"). Copied
verbatim from Tabler's outline set, one icon's <path> data per glyph — no
compiled bundle, no extraction script. Spec: docs/superpowers/specs/
2026-08-21-web-ui-visual-direction-design.md §6.2.
