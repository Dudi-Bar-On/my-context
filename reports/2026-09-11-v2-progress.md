## 10. DISTANCE TO PRODUCTION — and what the number does and does not mean

**There IS a defensible denominator, and it is the owner's own ruling.**
`DEC-v2-0-is-everything-still-open-and-the-in-out-cut-s-forty-six`, 2026-09-05: *"put all of them in
v2.0, so nothing remains out."* The 2026-09-04 in/out cut proposed excluding 46 tasks; he overturned
it. **So v2.0 is every open task, and the denominator is the `task` category itself.**

> **626 of 716 tracked tasks are done — 87%.**
> 90 open, 0 blocked, 20 open at priority 1. Superseded tasks (16) are excluded; deprecated ones
> (5 open) are not, because the standard says to skip only `superseded`.

**Three things make that number smaller than it looks, and all three should be read with it.**

1. **33 of the 90 close only when he looks.** They are not lane work at any rate of dispatch. The
   lane-able remainder is **57 tasks**.
2. **The denominator moves in both directions.** That same ruling of 2026-09-05 recorded *"the
   honest number after this ruling: 89 open tasks."* **Today it is 90.** Six days of closing — D33,
   D37, D38, D36, D43 and most of D41 among them — did not reduce the open count, because filing
   kept pace with closing. A percentage over a denominator that grows as fast as its numerator is a
   measure of *throughput*, not of *distance*. This is the single most important sentence in this
   report.
3. **`SHIPPED` here means "merged, and the test I ran is green", over 742 tests.** It does not mean
   the browser suite is green. Reported to this lane at dispatch and **not measured by it**: 41
   browser specs red against states the live corpus no longer holds, under an exception the owner
   approved this morning, and two `diagram-gate` tests red because D41 moved the CLI command count
   46 → 47 and `npm run gen:docs` has not been re-run. Both belong to other lanes; both are
   recorded here as their lanes' claims rather than as this report's measurement, and neither is
   counted in the 742.

**What this report will not do is convert that into a date, or into a single "% to production"
figure, and the reason is the point of the exercise.** The previous two instruments each asserted
one — the handover's **93%** and, before it, **96%** — and neither names a denominator anywhere.
Neither is reproducible. 93% is not 626/716, not 19/25, not 11/13, and not any ratio in this
document. It was remembered, not computed, which is exactly what rule 3 of this standard forbids.

**The three ratios that ARE computable, each answering a different question:**

| Question | Ratio | Figure |
|---|---|---|
| How much of the committed work is done? | 626 / 716 tasks | **87%** |
| How many numbered subjects are closed? | 19 / 25 Ds carrying items | **76%** |

## 11. THE STANDARD'S OWN EMBEDDED TABLE — what was done, and the question left for the owner

**Done today:** the instance inside
`STD-v2-0-progress-report-and-the-format-progress-reports-use` was replaced through
`mycontext edit`, so the table delivered at every session start is no longer three weeks old. The
rules — the five words, the citation obligation, computed counts, a regressed row saying so, and
displaying rather than filing — are untouched. They are the part of that item worth pinning.

**And the question this raises, put rather than answered, because it is his:** *should that standard
hold an instance at all?*

The argument for removing it is this project's own, from `CLAUDE.md`: **a copy cannot be superseded;
only the original can.** An instance embedded in a pinned item goes stale the next time a task
closes, and it goes stale *invisibly* — arriving every session with the authority of the rules
around it. That is precisely the defect this regeneration exists to correct, and it is the second
time it has been corrected by hand. The precedent is already in the corpus:
`STD-the-progress-table-has-one-format-and-this-is-it` holds **only** the format, names no numbers,
and has never gone stale.

The argument for keeping it is that a format nobody can see an example of is harder to obey, and the
embedded table is the example.

**Recommendation: hold the format, drop the instance, and let the standard name the newest dated
report in `reports/` instead** — name the item, do not quote it. That is a one-line edit he can make
or refuse, and this lane did not make it, because what a pinned item *contains* is a decision about
what governs, and that is his.
