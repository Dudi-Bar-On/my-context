# a lane was told to edit a file a standing owner ruling had frozen, and only the lane checked

Happened 2026-09-05, twice in one day, on docs/design/web-ui-mockup.html.

WHAT HAPPENED. DEC-the-mockup-is-a-frozen-reference-it-is-read-never-written has been active since
2026-09-02 and carries the owner word for word: "i told you not to change the mockup" / "not one
byte". Despite that, I dispatched one lane that edited the mockup and, hours later, a second lane
whose brief told it in writing to keep the mockup in step with the app. The second lane read the
corpus, found the ruling, refused the instruction and said so in its report. It was right and the
brief was wrong.

THE WORSE HALF IS NOT THE INSTRUCTION, IT IS THE QUESTION. walk/16 was put to the owner as "the
mockup carries emphasis the shipped English does not - which side moves?" with a recommendation to
reword the mockup. He answered in good faith. He was never told the mockup was frozen, so he could
not have known he was being asked to override his own standing ruling. A question that hides a
governing constraint does not collect a decision; it manufactures consent for one already made.

AND THE PREMISE DISSOLVES ON READING THE RULING. It says the product may run ahead of the drawing
WITHOUT THAT BEING A FAULT - only the mockup-ahead direction is a finding. So an app sentence
plainer than the mockup was never a defect at all, and walk/16 spent a lane resolving a
disagreement the record had already declared acceptable.

THE COST OF PUTTING IT RIGHT, stated because it is real: reverting restores a factual error to the
mockup - it says work.diffn is "word level" where lineDiff is a line-level LCS. A frozen reference
may hold an outdated statement; that is what frozen means. But the error is now in the record
rather than fixed, and someone reading the mockup will meet it.

WHAT TO DO INSTEAD. Read the corpus for what governs a FILE before writing a brief that touches it,
not after a lane objects. The check costs one query. And when a question to the owner touches
anything already ruled, name the ruling inside the question - his answer is only a decision if he
can see what he is deciding against.
