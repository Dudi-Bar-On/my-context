# a turn that is both a table and a lane report carries both marks; neither mark wins

Owner ruling at release checkpoint 2, 2026-09-22, on the recommendation of the dispatching session. Two rulings disagreed: the answer of 2026-09-16 to OPENQ-does-the-table-mark-or-the-lane-report-mark-win-when-one (two anchor types, two distinct marks, a turn that is both carries both; built under TASK-a-turn-that-is-both-a-table-and-a-lane-report-is-one-thing) and ruling G of 2026-09-21 (the lane report mark wins over the table mark). The 2026-09-16 answer stands: it was the more considered one, it is already built, and a precedence throws away information a reader might want. Ruling G is withdrawn on this point only; its other half - the anchor rebuild over the owner's archive runs in release phase 7 with MYCONTEXT_UI_SESSIONS_DIR sandboxing - stands.
