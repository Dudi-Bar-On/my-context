# Scope coverage: what the screen is, and what implemented means for it

WHAT THE SCREEN IS, so it can be built without opening the mockup. nav.inj -- Scope coverage, section data-p="coverage". Every path the walk reached, drawn as a tree and coloured by what governs it, with a detail pane beside the tree; and on each directory row a magnitude bar of three segments -- governed, examined but ungoverned, not examined -- written as shares of ONE denominator so they fill the track exactly, because a bar whose segments do not sum to the track reads as a measurement and is not one. The bar exists for the sentence the screen itself gives: four categorical dots said which rows were dark, and could not say how dark. Tree and detail both sit on a plate rather than on glass, because they are data. The endpoint answers only the paths the walk REACHED, so the not-examined share is zero on every row this screen can build; on a TRUNCATED walk that zero would be a lie, so the row declines to claim it and names the state in the screen's own two keyed words instead of inventing a number.

WHAT IMPLEMENTED MEANS: tree, detail pane and magnitude bar all drawn from the coverage endpoint; a truncated walk disclosed rather than painted as a complete one; nothing weaker drawn where the paths a truncated walk missed are still unknown -- and that last gap is real and open, held at plan:ui1 seq:17e. Disclosing that a walk stopped is not knowing where it stopped.
