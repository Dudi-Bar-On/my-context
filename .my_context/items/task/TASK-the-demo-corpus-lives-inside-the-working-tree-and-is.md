# the demo corpus lives inside the working tree and is underfoot in every search and screen

Owner ruling 2026-09-04: move it out. His words were that it just makes noise and confusion.

It is generated by scripts/demo-corpus.ts, is gitignored, and holds 818 files mirroring the
project’s own src, docs, test and scripts. Being a copy of the shape of the real project is
exactly what makes it confusing: a search hits two of everything and a screen drew its code as
though it were the project.

Making the file walk respect gitignore removes it from the screen and is tracked separately.
This item is the second half of the ruling: it should not be underfoot in an editor, a search
or a grep either.

102 files reference it. 34 are corpus items, 9 under src/ui, 7 under test/ui, plus the
generator, e2e specs that read its audit history, one under src/doctor, and documentation.
Every one must move or be updated; a half-moved fixture is worse than one left alone.

One reference is a RULE rather than a path, and it is the trap here. A corpus under test,
harness or demo-corpus is declared a fixture rather than a workspace, which is what stops a
nested corpus being mistaken for the one in force. That rule names the location. Move the
location and the rule must be re-decided rather than quietly broken, or a fixture becomes
findable as a workspace and the wrong corpus governs.

Decide and state where it goes, and why that place cannot be mistaken for the project either.
Outside the working tree entirely and a sibling directory are different answers with
different consequences for a fresh clone, for CI and for a contributor who has never
generated it.

What must be true at the end: the generator writes to the new place, every test and spec that
reads it passes unchanged in meaning, the fixture rule is honest about where fixtures live,
and a developer who has never run the generator sees a clear message rather than a confusing
absence.
