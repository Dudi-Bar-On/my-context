# a ruling taken in the screen walkthrough is captured before the next screen

OWNER DIRECTIVE, 2026-08-24, given during the proc walkthrough.

Every decision taken while walking the screens against the mockup is written into this corpus in the turn it is taken -- as a decision, a task, an open_question, a todo, or whatever category actually fits -- and not left in a conversation.

THE POINT IS THE REPORT AT THE END. Twenty-one screens and a hundred and eighty-two divergences produce more rulings than a person can hold, and the walkthrough is worth nothing if what it settled has to be reconstructed from a transcript afterwards. Captured as items, the state of the review is a QUERY: what was answered, what it was answered with, and what work each answer created.

WHAT THIS RULES OUT: summarising a screen s rulings in chat and capturing them later in a batch. A ruling recorded three screens later is recorded wrong or not at all, and the reasoning that made it obvious at the time is the first thing lost.

A ruling carries WHAT WAS WEIGHED AGAINST IT, not only what was chosen, so that none of them can be reopened from cost alone.
